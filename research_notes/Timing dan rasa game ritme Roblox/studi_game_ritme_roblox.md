# Case studies: rhythm games on Roblox (for Laskar Kendang)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Stats from Bloxodes were captured around early November 2026 (RoBeats' "best rank" timestamp reads Nov 5). Older information is flagged with its year. Several primary pages (Roblox Fandom wiki, TV Tropes, Pocket Tactics) blocked direct fetching; where a claim comes only from a search-result snippet of those pages, it is marked "(snippet)".

## 1. Which rhythm games on Roblox have been most popular, and what is their status in 2026?

### Takeaway
Funky Friday (Lyte Interactive, Feb 2021, ~2B visits) is the clear #1 in Roblox's "Music & Rhythm" subgenre, but by Nov 2026 it runs at only ~2.5K concurrent players. RoBeats! (2017, ~302M visits) and Sound Space (2018, ~44M visits) are long-lived but small (hundreds of CCU). The genre is a niche on Roblox. A new mobile-first "piano tiles" game (Sept 2026) briefly hit 12K CCU, which suggests casual, touch-first, social rhythm play can still break out. I found no Patapon-like or drum-command rhythm game on Roblox.

### Cited Findings
**Funky Friday (Friday Night Funkin'-style, 1v1 versus)**
- Creator Lyte Interactive. Created Feb 26, 2021. 2B visits, 4.1M favorites. 2.5K playing now, 24h peak 3.1K, 30-day average 2.5K, 30-day peak 5.8K. Rating 90.2%. Ranked #1 (current and best) in Music & Rhythm, #671 globally (best #299). Last updated "22 hours ago." — [Bloxodes: Funky Friday](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/funky-friday-2404080894)
- Visits passed 1.5B by mid-2023 — [Prima Games (June 2023)](https://primagames.com/codes/roblox-funky-friday-codes)
- A third-party tracker shows a peak of 24.3K CCU and 2B visits, with an undated snapshot — [Rotrends (snippet)](https://rotrends.com/game/2404080894)
- Based on Friday Night Funkin'. Players play FNF mods in multiplayer rooms, in 1v1 matches against other players in the same room — [fnfunkin.com](https://fnfunkin.com/roblox-funky-friday)
- RoWatcher's 2026 feature says launch-hype peak CCU exceeded 100,000. It says typical CCU is 15K–35K (about 30K on a typical day) and the Discord has over 300K members. It also credits "Lsplash" with an April 2021 release and describes an in-game chart creator with a public library of "tens of thousands" of charts — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/funky-friday-roblox-s-rhythm-king-still-packs-the-dance-floor). **Low reliability:** the CCU figures conflict with Bloxodes (2.5K CCU in Nov 2026), the release date conflicts with the Feb 26, 2021 creation date, and the chart-creator claim is not corroborated anywhere else I found.

**RoBeats! Music + Rhythm + RPG (4-key, 3D lane, score attack)**
- Creator RobeatsDev. Created Mar 15, 2017. 301.7M visits, 1M favorites. 287 playing now. 30-day average 291.9 (−12.5%), 30-day peak 441. Rating 80.1%. Music & Rhythm rank #8 (best #5). Last updated 7 days ago — [Bloxodes: RoBeats](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/robeats-music-rhythm-rpg-301252049)
- The developer is spotco, publishing as RobeatsDev. It is described as a 4-key rhythm game, "like osu!mania … but in 3D" — [search snippet summarizing TV Tropes / wikiwiki.jp](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/Robeats)
- The Korean wiki (revised 2026-10-03) gives average concurrents of 400–1000 and lists PC, mobile and console as platforms. It says the game started as a doujin (indie) game and was last updated Jan 17, 2026 at the time of writing — [Namuwiki: Robeats!](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **March 24, 2026:** RoBeats went offline ("Experience currently unavailable"). Speculation blamed an artist's copyright dispute with the developer, unconfirmed. spotco pointed players to a "RoBeats Alt" server with reset data and said the team was "working hard to bring the original game back online" — [PiunikaWeb, Mar 24, 2026](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/03/24/roblox-players-react-as-robeats-goes-down-and-classic-faces-removed-from-sale/)
- Namuwiki says the game was deleted on "March 23" by Roblox-side moderation and deleted again about 3 months after being restored. No year is given — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- A June 2026 guide calls RoBeats "the closest thing the platform has to a dedicated rhythm-game app" — [GamertagMythras, Jun 23, 2026](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)

**Sound Space (osu!/"Rhythm Space"-style 3D cursor aim)**
- Creator Sound Space Studio. Created Dec 25, 2018. 43.6M visits, 434.5K favorites. 92 playing now, 30-day average 150.1 (−19%), peak 262. Rating 85.9%. Music & Rhythm rank #9. Updated 43 hours before capture — [Bloxodes: Sound Space](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/sound-space-rhythm-game-964540701)
- The original developer (Chedski) says "When the absolute dumpster fire known as the audio update happened, the game closed." They built Sound Space Plus as a standalone desktop game, now marked "Canceled," MIT-licensed, with builds dated Nov 17, 2022 — [Chedski itch.io](https://chedski.itch.io/sound-space-plus)
- The standalone lineage became "Rhythia." A Steam Early Access version by Capo Games (a two-person team, $3.99) released Feb 20, 2026. A review notes the community disputes which version is "official" — [Steam: Rhythia](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2250500) (details partly via search snippet)
- Described as needing a mouse. Praised as scratching "the osu! itch," but its 3D-aim style is called "genuinely an acquired taste" — [GamertagMythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)

**Friday Night Bloxxin' (FNF-style versus)**
- Reported as discontinued after its developers faced doxxing and harassment (article undated in results) — [Pocket Tactics (snippet)](https://www.pockettactics.com/roblox/friday-night-bloxxin-discontinued)
- A tracker shows 213 players and an "all-time peak" of 504 (May 2026). That peak likely reflects only the tracker's own window — [RoWatcher (snippet)](https://rowatcher.com/games/2953945619/friday-night-bloxxin)

**Piano / tiles games (casual, mostly mobile)**
- "Piano Rhythm Tiles" (creator "Music Tiles") was released Sept 2026. It has 23.6M visits and 426K favorites, with about 1K online at capture and an all-time peak of 12K CCU in October 2026. Features: tap tiles in time with the music, chosen speed, Endless mode with rising speed, 1v1 challenges, a Daily Song, a Fade (memory) mode, unlockable tile skins and backgrounds, stars/personal bests/leaderboards, and "touch, keyboard, or controller" input — [RoWatcher: Piano Rhythm Tiles](https://rowatcher.com/games/10766845655/piano-tiles)
- "Digital Piano" (a free-play instrument, not a rhythm game) has about 57.6M visits and a built-in MIDI autoplayer — [RoWatcher: Digital Piano](https://rowatcher.com/games/3349588628/digital-piano)

**Other titles mentioned in listicles:** Sound Volblox and Rhythm Master (an anime-song rhythm game). No stats were found — [West Games](https://west-games.com/roblox-rhythm-games/)

**Beat Saber-likes:** One low-quality source claims Sound Space was originally called "Blox Saber." I found no reliable source for a significant Beat Saber-style Roblox game (search snippet; unreliable).

### Inferences
- Even the #1 Music & Rhythm game sits around global rank #671 with ~2.5K CCU. On Roblox, rhythm is a small niche compared with action and simulator games. Laskar Kendang should not expect pure-rhythm virality. Its squad, co-op and progression layer is what gives it Roblox-native hooks.
- The biggest successes (Funky Friday, Piano Rhythm Tiles) are social: 1v1 versus, rooms, daily songs. Solo score-attack games (RoBeats, Sound Space) keep loyal but small audiences.
- Funky Friday's peak was riding the FNF craze (2021). Borrowed IP gave it a fast start but also song removals (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No official Roblox numbers. All stats are third-party snapshots (Bloxodes, RoWatcher, Rotrends). Funky Friday's historical peak CCU is unverified (24.3K vs ">100K" claims).
- I could not confirm whether RoBeats' March 2026 takedown was copyright-related. The Nov 2026 Bloxodes page shows the original place (created 2017) active again, but I found no restoration announcement.
- No reliable data on Beat Saber-style or VR rhythm games on Roblox.

## 2. How do they handle music sync, hit windows, calibration/offset, mobile controls and scroll speed?

### Takeaway
Public detail is thin. Successful games expose scroll/note speed, rebindable keys and (Funky Friday) an offset setting, and RoBeats uses a deliberately generous 4-tier judgement. DevForum consensus is to drive note timing from the Sound's `TimePosition`, read every frame, minus a player offset, rather than from wall-clock timers. No developer has published exact hit-window millisecond values for these games.

### Cited Findings
- **RoBeats judgement:** PERFECT / GREAT / OKAY / MISS. Judging is "fairly generous," and releasing a long note early can still give OKAY. Score multiplier = 1 + combo×0.01, fixed at 2× past 100 combo. OKAY does not add to combo. A MISS halves the current combo instead of resetting it to 0 (2026-10 revision; an earlier revision said it resets to 0) — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **RoBeats note speed:** adjustable in settings only since August 2020. Before that it changed only through gear upgrades. Community key layouts include "ASL;", "QWOP" and "120-" — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **RoBeats pay-to-win timing:** some gears widen the PERFECT window, so one player gets PERFECT on the same timing where others don't. This is listed as a player complaint — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **Funky Friday scroll speed:** default 1. Guides advise small increments — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/what-s-best-speed-roblox-funky-friday)
- **Funky Friday keybinds:** a settings gear (top right) has a Keybinds section listing lanes left to right; D-F-J-K is suggested — [Earnaldo (low reliability)](https://earnaldo.com/blog/funky-friday-free-robux-guide)
- **Funky Friday offset:** a blog says the game lets you adjust offset "until your hits feel locked to the beat"; the menu location is unconfirmed — [oembed.org blog (low reliability)](https://oembed.org/blog/funky-friday-optimize-your-osu)
- **Funky Friday judgements and options:** hits rated Perfect/Good/Bad/Miss, a health bar that drains on misses, scroll speed, mirror mode, a ghost-tap penalty toggle, and touch controls on mobile — [RoWatcher (low reliability, see Q1)](https://rowatcher.com/news/funky-friday-roblox-s-rhythm-king-still-packs-the-dance-floor)
- **Mobile ceiling:** a 2026 guide says mobile works for all the listed games but "limits the ceiling on the hardest charts." Keyboard suits lane games and Sound Space "wants a mouse" — [GamertagMythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)
- **Sync technique (DevForum):** timers drift because audio can be delayed or freeze with client performance. Instead, derive song position from `Sound.TimePosition` minus a start reference and a first-beat offset, recomputed every frame on Heartbeat — [DevForum: Syncing events with music](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/syncing-events-with-music/1538437)
- **Judging pattern (DevForum):** on each press, find the nearest upcoming or previous note and compare its ideal time with the press time. One suggestion: let the client show feedback immediately while the server reads the time — [DevForum: Predicting notes](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/predicting-notes-on-a-track-for-a-rhythm-game/1116727)
- **RhythmService** (a community module) models judgements as tolerance bands in seconds (GoalTime vs HitTime) and returns the closest tolerance's rating, or nil if outside all bands — [DevForum: RhythmService documentation](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rhythmservice-documentation/1135625)
- **Layered tracks:** repeatedly setting a secondary track's `TimePosition` to match the primary stopped working reliably. The posted workaround nudges `PlaybackSpeed` up or down when drift exceeds a small threshold — [DevForum feature request](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **Input latency (older, desktop):** a DevForum thread reported an input queue that could delay `UserInputService` events by about 400ms or more. One user fixed it by lowering the FPS cap from 240 to 60 — [DevForum: Significant input delay](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/significant-input-delay-camera-lag/344960)
- **Difficulty (2019):** "Rhythm games are extremely complicated and hard to get the hang of" — a DevForum commenter who skimmed RoBeats' open mapping-kit code — [DevForum: How to code a rhythm game](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-code-a-rhythm-game-official/227339)

### Inferences
- A generous judgement plus a soft miss penalty (RoBeats halves the combo) keeps casual and mobile players engaged. For Laskar Kendang, a missed beat could weaken the command or reduce the combo instead of wiping all progress.
- Never sell timing advantages. RoBeats' window-widening gears are a recurring complaint.
- Ship from day one: an offset/calibration screen, note or beat-indicator speed, rebindable keys, and large touch drum pads.

### Gaps
- No published millisecond hit windows for Funky Friday or RoBeats, and no developer interviews on sync architecture.
- No first-party detail on how either game lays out mobile touch controls (lane tap zones vs. buttons) or handles mobile latency.

## 3. What do players complain about and praise?

### Takeaway
Praise centers on easy-to-learn, hard-to-master play, versus or social play (Funky Friday), and deep libraries with real difficulty (RoBeats). Complaints center on pay-to-win progression (RoBeats gears, VIP), grindy events, server disconnects losing progress, song removals and copyright takedowns, a lower mobile skill ceiling, and harassment in toxic communities (Friday Night Bloxxin').

### Cited Findings
- **Funky Friday praise:** called "the definitive Friday Night Funkin' experience on the platform" and the most fun in a group. Criticism: "a little thin" for solo score-chasing — [GamertagMythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)
- **Genre split:** Roblox rhythm games "split into two camps: FNF-style note-mashers built for trash talk, and clean tap-the-circle charters built for actual scores," described as "two genres wearing the same coat" — [GamertagMythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)
- **RoBeats complaints:**
  - Heavy gear dependence; upgrade boosters, protection items and legendary gears widen the gap between new or low-spend players and others.
  - Alt accounts mass-farming tokens.
  - Difficulty inflation (levels 29+ vary widely).
  - The RB Battles event was long and pushed VIP purchases.
  - Server disconnects lost unsaved progress.
  - [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **RoBeats praise:** RB Battles was well received through Season 2. The gear system is credited as a success factor because skill differences don't fully dominate scores. The community server offers varied charts — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **RoBeats as a solo grind:** competition happens through leaderboards, not live head-to-head play — [GamertagMythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)
- **Takedown reactions:** "THEY TOOK DOWN ROBEATS NOOOOO" when the game went offline in March 2026; players lost access to years of progress, and the Alt server reset data — [PiunikaWeb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/03/24/roblox-players-react-as-robeats-goes-down-and-classic-faces-removed-from-sale/)
- **Funky Friday song removals:** many base FNF songs were removed "for either copyright or the behaviour of the original owner." Some mods returned in a 2023 Christmas update — [Roblox Fandom wiki (snippet)](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Lyte_Interactive/Funky_Friday)
- **Agoti mod removal (~2021):** Lyte said on Discord that it removed the Agoti mod over controversy about the mod's creator, not copyright — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/why-was-the-agoti-mod-removed-from-roblox-funky-friday-developer-responds/)
- **Friday Night Bloxxin':** reportedly discontinued after developers faced online and physical harassment — [Pocket Tactics (snippet)](https://www.pockettactics.com/roblox/friday-night-bloxxin-discontinued)
- **2022 audio update sentiment:** Sound Space's creator called it "the absolute dumpster fire known as the audio update" — [Chedski itch.io](https://chedski.itch.io/sound-space-plus). DevForum posters called the rollout a mess and said audio they had relied on for years vanished — [DevForum: Update to audio privacy](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717)

### Inferences
- For a co-op PvE game, the "group fun" praise for Funky Friday is the closest analog. Players value playing with friends more than chart depth.
- Do not attach long-term progress to licensed third-party music. When songs are removed, progress tied to them disappears and players get angry.

### Gaps
- I could not access Reddit or Discord threads directly. Mobile-specific complaints (input lag, touch accuracy) for Funky Friday and RoBeats are not documented in sources I could reach.

## 4. Effect of the 2022 audio privacy update and later licensing changes; how games source music now

### Takeaway
Roblox's copyright tightening came in waves: mid-2018 copyright flagging, the March 22, 2022 privacy change (audio over 6 seconds made private unless uploaded by the experience owner), and from Sept 2024 a licensed DistroKid catalog plus Audible Magic scanning. Survivors either licensed music (RoBeats paid indie and J-core artists), dropped or replaced songs (Funky Friday), or left the platform (Sound Space to standalone). Original music uploaded by the experience owner is the safe path.

### Cited Findings
- **2018 wave:** early RoBeats used copyrighted songs. In mid-2018 Roblox stopped allowing them. spotco replaced them with cover versions ("REMIXbeats") — [TV Tropes (snippet)](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/Robeats). Related DevForum threads: [Music Copyright Flagging is Live Today](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/music-copyright-flagging-is-live-today/137494); [Upcoming Music Changes and Copyright Related Action](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-music-changes-and-copyright-related-action/130570)
- **RoBeats licensing phase:** about mid-Dec 2020 to mid-Aug 2021. Every song not licensed for a for-profit game was replaced with songs from artists spotco licensed; pre-phase songs were removed unless permission was obtained — [wikiwiki.jp (snippet)](https://wikiwiki.jp/robeats/)
- **RoBeats licensed artists:** Camellia (かめりあ), xi, LeaF, t+pazolite, RiraN, EmoCosine, Creo and Geoxor, paid via license fees. Artists are added through "featuring" events, mostly Japanese, plus artists from Korea, the US, Canada, France, Latvia, Greece and Austria — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **2022 privacy change:** new audio uploads became private from March 22, 2022, and existing audio longer than 6 seconds became private retroactively. The official licensed Music/SFX catalog stayed public — [DevForum: Upcoming Changes to Asset Privacy for Audio](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/action-needed-upcoming-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1701697)
- **Ownership rule:** audio stops working if it wasn't uploaded by the same user or group that owns the experience — [Gamertweak](https://gamertweak.com/why-roblox-remove-audios/)
- **Lawsuit background:** the change is tied to a 2021 lawsuit over user-uploaded copyrighted songs — [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/2022/06/how-to-play-music-in-roblox/)
- **False positives:** licensed Monstercat tracks were flagged and later restored — [DevForum: Monstercat audio no longer playable](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/audio-part-of-the-monstercat-deal-is-no-longer-playable-decoy-world/1692417). Users also reported removals of CC-BY songs — [DevForum: CC-BY removals](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-removing-songs-licensed-under-creative-commons-attribution/2559774)
- **Effect on open-source projects:** spotco's open RoBeats template says the bundled track "won't work by default when you copy the place" until Roblox releases cross-experience audio permissions. The bundled Monday Night Monsters soundtrack is offered as a free download — [DevForum: RoBeats template](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/open-source-robeats-rhythm-game-custom-server-template/824563)
- **Sound Space** closed after the audio update; its creator moved to a standalone game where players load their own songs, avoiding moderation and copyright issues — [Chedski itch.io](https://chedski.itch.io/sound-space-plus)
- **Sept 2024 DistroKid integration:** independent artists' music is available in the Creator Store audio tab (web and Studio Toolbox). Rights holders could upload via DistroKid from October 2024. All audio passes through Audible Magic and matches are blocked automatically. Groups could not acquire Creator Store audio assets at the time — [DevForum: Amplify your experiences with new music](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/amplify-your-experiences-with-new-music/3164792)
- About 180,000 DistroKid tracks were added since Sept 2024. A Music Top 100 chart ranks licensed, ISRC-tagged tracks by playtime and adoption; partners include DistroKid, Monstercat, APM and Clippsly — [Music Week](https://musicweek.com/digital/read/roblox-launches-top-100-chart-featuring-music-on-the-platform/091037); [DevForum: Music Top 100 Chart](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/beta-introducing-the-music-top-100-chart-on-roblox/3301659)
- **Streamer concerns:** DevForum commenters warned that copyright-matched music can get streamers struck and suggested a mute-music option — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/amplify-your-experiences-with-new-music/3164792)
- **Funky Friday sourcing:** FNF songs plus community-made songs — [fnfunkin.com](https://fnfunkin.com/roblox-funky-friday). Many base FNF songs were later removed (see Q3).

### Inferences
- Laskar Kendang already renders original soundfont music (commit d680647). That is the right call: original gamelan or kendang tracks uploaded by the experience owner avoid every failure mode above.
- If using DistroKid or Creator Store tracks, upload or acquire them under the same owner (user or group) as the experience. Even licensed music can be pulled through artist disputes (RoBeats 2026).
- Original drum loops under 6 seconds were never affected by the privacy rule, but the safe principle is still to own all audio.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement on whether RoBeats' 2026 takedown was a DMCA or artist dispute.
- No details on whether Funky Friday re-licensed any FNF music or how Lyte handles audio ownership after 2022.

## 5. Monetization and retention patterns

### Takeaway
Roblox rhythm games monetize mainly through soft currency plus cosmetics: points or coins bought with Robux, animations, microphones, skins and note skins. They add multiplier gamepasses, VIP subscriptions with exclusive songs, and gacha-style machines. Retention leans on versus matches, weekly leaderboards, seasonal events, daily songs and codes. The pay-to-win elements in RoBeats drew criticism.

### Cited Findings
- **Funky Friday points:** max 50 per song based on accuracy (100 with the Double Points gamepass, 399 Robux). Points are awarded if the opponent backs out. Point bundles run from 150 points for 49 Robux to 25,000 points for 999 Robux (undated) — [Roblox Fandom wiki (snippet)](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Lyte_Interactive/Funky_Friday)
- **Funky Friday spending:** points buy animations and microphones at "Bom's shop" — [Outsider Gaming](https://outsidergaming.com/funky-friday-roblox-codes/); [Fandom (snippet)](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Lyte_Interactive/Funky_Friday)
- **Funky Friday codes:** codes still give points, microphones and animations in 2026. One May 2026 code was worth 500 points — [Pocket Tactics codes](https://www.pockettactics.com/funky-friday/codes); [iGeeksBlog (Aug 2026)](https://www.igeeksblog.com/roblox-funky-friday-codes/)
- Cosmetics (characters, note skins, UI themes) are sold for Robux, and gameplay itself is not paywalled — [RoWatcher (low reliability)](https://rowatcher.com/news/funky-friday-roblox-s-rhythm-king-still-packs-the-dance-floor)
- **RoBeats VIP:** Bronze 1 month for 450 Robux, Silver 3 months for 1250 Robux, Gold 6 months for 2250 Robux. Benefits: double coins, VIP-only songs, a VIP title. Roblox Premium members get a 3-day free trial — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **RoBeats currencies:** coins and stars are bought with Robux (100 Robux ≈ 2250 coins). Stars buy HARD songs, specific gears and retired songs. The Note Machine gacha costs 100 coins or 1 star per pull. Boosts and boxes are also sold — [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **RoBeats retention loops:**
  - Multiplayer servers hold up to 100 players. First place earns 5–30 coins depending on lobby size.
  - Spectators who press a "cheer" button earn 5 coins for themselves and 5 for the player they watch.
  - "Compete" is a weekly leaderboard on 4 random songs, with a 500-coin entry fee and rewards of titles and blueprints.
  - Gear crafting and upgrades.
  - [Namuwiki](https://namu.moe/w/Robeats!)
- **Piano Rhythm Tiles (2026):** Daily Song, 1v1 challenges, Endless mode, unlockable tile skins and backgrounds, stars and leaderboards — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10766845655/piano-tiles)
- **Open template:** spotco's RoBeats template includes a "one-step hook" for selling a game pass and DataStore code for settings and recent plays — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/open-source-robeats-rhythm-game-custom-server-template/824563)
- **Genre norm:** Roblox rhythm games "typically sell optional Robux cosmetics, character skins, or convenience unlocks" — [GamertagMythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/roblox/best-roblox-music-games)

### Inferences
- For Laskar Kendang, the safe model is cosmetic: drum skins, soldier uniforms, banners, beat-hit effects, victory dances. Add a soft currency from stage clears and possibly a currency-multiplier pass.
- RoBeats' spectator "cheer" reward (both players get coins) and Funky Friday's points-on-opponent-leave are cheap social incentives that fit co-op well, for example rewarding allies for completing commands together.
- A Daily Stage or weekly challenge with a leaderboard (RoBeats Compete, Piano Tiles Daily Song) suits a 4-command rhythm game where chart authoring is cheap.

### Gaps
- No revenue figures for any Roblox rhythm game.
- No confirmed battle-pass implementation in Funky Friday or RoBeats in the sources found.

## 6. Rhythm + strategy hybrids or command-based rhythm games on Roblox

### Takeaway
I found no Roblox game that implements Patapon-style rhythm commands for a squad. The only Patapon-related Roblox item found is a character roleplay or morph experience. Rhythm-strategy hybrids exist off-platform (Ratatan, Wardrum, itch.io jam games). This is unoccupied space on Roblox, with unproven demand.

### Cited Findings
- **"Patapon DonChaka RP":** a Roblox roleplay with 28 Patapon morphs. The creator says they can't update it and blames Roblox. There is no rhythm-command gameplay — [Roblox profile page](https://www.roblox.com/users/566123420/profile)
- DevForum searches for rhythm-strategy, drum or army Roblox games returned no matching project. The closest results were a story pitch "Of Beats and Shapes" and an unrelated strategy game — [DevForum: Of Beats and Shapes](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-game-idea-of-beats-and-shapes/456835)

## 7. Open-source Roblox rhythm frameworks and what they reveal about architecture

### Takeaway
The main open-source references come from the RoBeats lineage: spotco's 2020 template (Rojo, song select, DataStore, gamepass hook, mapping place) and the community RoBeats CS (Roact UI, a web-server leaderboard with Redis). There are also smaller modules such as RhythmService and a roblox-ts "music-hero" project. The common architecture: the client owns the clock and judgement, maps are separate data with a build step, and a server or DataStore persists settings and scores.

### Cited Findings
- **spotco template (Oct 17, 2020):** "We're open-sourcing our rhythm game engine with tools and additional code to make it easy!" It is "100% Free and open source to do anything you want with." Includes:
  - song-select code and menus
  - DataStore code for saved settings and recent plays
  - a one-step game-pass hook
  - Rojo support
  - an uncopylocked mapping place
  - A commenter says the template may be deprecated.
  - [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/open-source-robeats-rhythm-game-custom-server-template/824563)
- **Template repo:** built with Rojo 6.0.0-rc.1. Folders: `ReplicatedStorage`, `ServerScriptService/GameServer`, `StarterPlayer` and `Workspace`, plus `selene.toml` and `.luacheckrc`. 115 commits, 8 stars, no LICENSE file visible (despite the "free to use" statement) — [GitHub: spotco/RobeatsCustomServerTemplate](https://github.com/spotco/RobeatsCustomServerTemplate)
- **RoBeats CS (community fork, June 2020):** a full rewrite with UI in Roact (React-like), an osu!-like gameplay screen, results-screen metrics, and spectating. Leaderboards, global rankings and skill rating were slow, so a Redis cache in the web server was planned — [dev.to: RoBeats CS Rewrite](https://dev.to/regen_erate/robeats-cs-rewrite-50nl). Repos include [RoVSRG/robeats-cs](https://github.com/RoVSRG/robeats-cs) and a separate maps repo with a custom build system (per search snippet).
- **RhythmService:** a DevForum module where judgements are tolerance bands in seconds — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rhythmservice-documentation/1135625)
- **cataclysmic-studios/music-hero:** a Roblox game "inspired by Fortnite Festival," written in roblox-ts (TypeScript) — [ecosyste.ms listing](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fcataclysmic-studios%2Fmusic-hero)
- **Sound Space Plus** (standalone, not Roblox) is MIT-licensed with source on GitHub — [Chedski itch.io](https://chedski.itch.io/sound-space-plus)

### Inferences
- Common pattern for Laskar Kendang:
  1. Keep chart and beat data as plain module tables, separate from gameplay code.
  2. Run judgement on the client against the audio clock with a per-player offset.
  3. Send only command results (command type, accuracy, combo) to the server, which validates plausibility and drives the shared squad and enemy state for co-op.
  4. Persist settings (offset, keybinds, speed) in DataStore.
- RoBeats CS's slow leaderboards are a warning: keep ranked or leaderboard features simple (OrderedDataStore) and avoid external web servers. HttpService also needs permission under project rules.

### Gaps
- I could not read source files to confirm specific module names, hit-window constants or the exact clock method in the RoBeats template or RoBeats CS.
- License status of the RoBeats repos is unclear; check each repo's LICENSE before reusing any code.
