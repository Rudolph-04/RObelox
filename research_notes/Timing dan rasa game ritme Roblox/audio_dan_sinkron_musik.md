# Roblox audio playback for rhythm games: music sync and drum-hit latency (state as of Nov 2026)

Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[DOC]**: official Roblox Creator Docs (fetched from the docs' source repo `Roblox/creator-docs`, which is what create.roblox.com renders).
- **[STAFF]**: statement by a Roblox engineer on the DevForum. ReallyLongArms, DinoCoderMom, HereIsRich, Focia19 and PeacefulSquirrel write as the Roblox audio team ("we rolled back...", "we stream..."). The fetched pages did not always show staff badges, so for some of them the staff role is inferred from wording.
- **[MEASURED]**: community measurement with numbers.
- **[ANECDOTE]**: community report without measurement.
- **[SNIPPET]**: seen only in a search-result snippet, page not fetched. Lower confidence.
- Dates matter. Roblox audio behavior changed several times in 2025-2026, and anything from before 2024 may be outdated.

Context assumed: Laskar Kendang, 120 BPM (0.5 s/beat), a beat clock from `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` plus a server start attribute, a 16 s looped `Sound` checked once per second and snapped back if drift is over 30 ms, a parallel "Semangat" loop at volume 0 that crossfades in, and drum hits as short `Sound`s in SoundService (`TimePosition = 0; :Play()`).

## 1. How accurate is Sound.TimePosition for reading and seeking? Update granularity, drift, glitches on seek?

### Takeaway
Roblox officially offers **no timing or sync guarantees** for legacy `Sound`. The audio mixer runs on its own thread, and scripts only touch it once per visual frame. A `Play()` or seek takes effect on the next "mixer step". Seeking (setting `TimePosition`) on medium or large files forces a **re-buffer**, which can stutter or start late. Several 2025-2026 regressions made frequent TimePosition writes glitchy. The documented update granularity of TimePosition is not published. Snapping music by writing `TimePosition` is therefore the riskiest part of the current Laskar design.

### Cited Findings
- **[DOC]** TimePosition "can be changed to move the playback position of the sound both before and during playback". It advances at the `PlaybackSpeed` rate. On a looped sound, setting it past the length "will not cause it to wrap around". `TimePosition`, `PlaybackSpeed`, `TimeLength` and `IsLoaded` are tagged NotReplicated. The docs say nothing about precision or update rate. — [Sound.yaml (creator-docs)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Sound.yaml); [Sound reference](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Sound)
- **[STAFF, Jul 7 2025]** ReallyLongArms: "we don't offer any strong timing or synchronization guarantees". "The audio engine runs concurrently with scripts", and scripts get a chance to change things "every _visual_ frame". `:Play` is not guaranteed to take effect instantly. `Track2.TimePosition = Track1.TimePosition` reads a value that is already stale by the time it is written, because Track1 keeps playing. A frequently re-seeked track "gets a frame or so to start playing before it has to seek to a new location", which causes the glitching. — [Syncing Sounds with TimePosition isn't reliable anymore](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/syncing-sounds-with-timeposition-isnt-reliable-anymore-glitches-and-stuttery-sounds/3800504)
- **[STAFF, Mar 13 2026]** Roblox loads audio with 3 strategies: (1) short files are fully decompressed in RAM and seeking is nearly instant, (2) medium files stay compressed in RAM and are streamed and decompressed asynchronously, (3) large files are streamed from disk. For (2) and (3), "changing TimePosition requires re-buffering" before playback resumes. The size thresholds "have been adjusted over the years" (no numbers published). — [Allow Us to Continuously Sync TimePosition of 2+ Sound Objects](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[STAFF, Apr 15 2026]** `Play()` calls on separate sounds "can straddle a mixer tick", so two sounds started on the same script line can start one mixer step apart. — [same thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[STAFF, Oct 13 2026]** Each `:Play` "takes effect on the next 'mixer step'". "The mixer is constantly running in the background while your scripts execute". "Fundamentally our system didn't provide any guarantees even if you pre-seek". "Updating the TimePosition shortly before playing can make the timing gap worse", because the file may need to buffer. — [Add a way to sync audio tracks together](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-way-to-sync-audio-tracks-together/2446731)
- **[STAFF, Mar 20 2025]** A March 2025 engine change "inadvertently made setting `TimePosition` slower". It broke piano games that seek inside multi-note files, and Roblox rolled it back on Mar 20, 2025. — [Sound TimePosition inaccuracies](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sound-timeposition-inaccuracies/3555246); [Heavy sound/input lag in piano games?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/heavy-soundinput-lag-in-piano-games/3548917)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jan 17 2026 and May 26 2026]** The piano-game lag reportedly returned. In May 2026 it was described as slight on desktop but "extremely noticeable" on mobile. The thread shows no staff response to the recurrence. — [Heavy sound/input lag in piano games?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/heavy-soundinput-lag-in-piano-games/3548917)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jul 2025 and Mar 2026]** Repeatedly copying a primary track's TimePosition onto a secondary track "worked perfectly" before early 2025. Since then the secondary track "stutters or glitches when updated frequently, even in older projects". — [3800504](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/syncing-sounds-with-timeposition-isnt-reliable-anymore-glitches-and-stuttery-sounds/3800504); [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jul 2023]** Tracks preloaded with ContentProvider stay in sync only when started from TimePosition 0. After a seek or pause/resume they audibly desync, yet the printed TimePosition values are equal. TimePosition readouts do not necessarily reflect what is audible. — [Add a way to sync audio tracks together](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-way-to-sync-audio-tracks-together/2446731)
- **[MEASURED, Apr 15 2026]** One developer saw a third track lag **16-50 ms** behind the others after seeking all of them to 41.2 s. — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[MEASURED, 2022, fixed]** Server-side upload processing appended a random amount of silence to audio. A clip went from 0.6857 s to 0.6879 s (+2.2 ms) and a later download measured +12.3 ms. Two loops that were equal at upload differed by about 22 ms. Staff said it was fixed on May 12, 2022 and asked for re-uploads. Before the fix, developers had compensated with manual TimePosition offsets. — [Recent Changes to Uploaded Audio Compression/Processing Causing Timing Drift](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recent-changes-to-uploaded-audio-compressionprocessing-causing-timing-drift/1735440)
- **[SNIPPET]** TimePosition is a double, so equality comparisons against note times fail. — [How would you make accuracy system](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-would-you-make-accuracy-system/1279543). TimePosition fires no change event, so `Changed` is not useful, and it stays 0 in Studio preview for looped sounds (bug report). — [TimePosition not updating the interface while sound is Looped](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/timeposition-not-updating-the-interface-while-sound-is-looped/2043001)
- **[SNIPPET, old, likely outdated]** Seeking some audio files once stopped playback for a few seconds before it resumed at the target. — [Audio is not loading/playing properly](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/audio-is-not-loading-playing-properly/362896)

### Inferences
- A script can only read or write TimePosition at frame boundaries, and the mixer applies changes at its own step boundaries. Any single read therefore carries roughly one frame of uncertainty: 16.7 ms at 60 FPS, 33 ms at 30 FPS, plus an unknown mixer-step size. **Laskar's 30 ms snap threshold is at or below this noise floor on low-FPS devices (common on mobile)**, so it can trigger false snaps.
- Each snap is a seek. If the 16 s track falls into loading strategy 2 or 3 (thresholds unknown), every snap re-buffers, which risks a click, gap or stutter, and the post-snap position can itself be off. The current "check every second, snap if over 30 ms" loop could make the music worse than leaving it alone.
- The second "Semangat" loop is the exact "sync 2+ Sound objects" case that Roblox staff say legacy `Sound` cannot guarantee. Snapping it independently each second could make the two loops drift apart audibly (flanging or phasing during the crossfade).

### Gaps
- No official number for how often TimePosition updates (per frame, per mixer buffer, or coarser), or for the mixer step or buffer size.
- No published file-size or duration thresholds for the three loading strategies, so it is unknown whether a 16 s track is "short" (seek nearly instant) or "medium" (re-buffer on seek).
- No published measurement of long-term drift between TimePosition and wall-clock time (os.clock or GetServerTimeNow).
- No source confirms whether a single seek produces an audible click. Reports describe stutter and glitches under frequent seeks.

## 2. Typical output latency from Sound:Play() to audible sound on PC, Mac, mobile and console

### Takeaway
**No official or measured per-platform output-latency numbers exist for Roblox.** All available evidence is anecdotal: about 100 ms on some clip playbacks (2024), clone-related delays mostly in Studio (2025-2026), mobile worse than desktop (2026), and players reporting 40-100 ms offsets (2026, cause unknown). Structurally, every script-triggered sound waits for the frame boundary, then the next mixer step, then the OS or device output buffer.

### Cited Findings
- **[DOC]** The Sound, SoundService and AudioPlayer references do not mention latency at all. — [Sound.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Sound.yaml); [SoundService.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SoundService.yaml); [AudioPlayer.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/AudioPlayer.yaml)
- **[STAFF, 2025-2026]** Pipeline facts: scripts change audio state once per visual frame, and `:Play` lands on the next mixer step. — [3800504](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/syncing-sounds-with-timeposition-isnt-reliable-anymore-glitches-and-stuttery-sounds/3800504); [2446731](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-way-to-sync-audio-tracks-together/2446731)
- **[STAFF, Jun 28 2023]** "Sound.Ended actually happens on the first frame after the sound has ended". There was no good way to play sounds at exact times, because scripts run on frame intervals. — [Sound.PlaybackRegionsEnabled ... do nothing](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/soundplaybackregionsenabled-soundplaybackregion-soundloopregion-do-nothing/2440706)
- **[ANECDOTE, May 2024]** Trimmed clips started about **0.1 s** late. The poster first blamed server-side clone-and-destroy and moved playback to the client (`SoundService:PlayLocalSound` via a remote). Later they said the delay only showed in Studio, not the live game. No staff reply. — [Playing a sound ... have a ~0.1 second delay](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/playing-a-sound-by-any-means-and-playonremove-have-a-01-second-delay/2961664)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jun 11 2026]** After `GetMixerTime` shipped, some players of one rhythm game reported audio offsets of about **40-100 ms**. Staff said there were "no known changes" to regular `Sound` or unscheduled `AudioPlayer` Play/Stop and asked for video. The developer switched to `AudioPlayer:Play(atTime)` and one player reported improvement, possibly placebo. — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[ANECDOTE, May 2026]** Piano-game sound lag is "slight on desktop" and "extremely noticeable on mobile". — [3548917](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/heavy-soundinput-lag-in-piano-games/3548917)
- **[ANECDOTE, 2022]** A piano-player developer saw lag only on the first press of each key. The suggested fix was preloading, so the asset isn't downloaded at first play. — [Sound Delay Latency (Local)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sound-delay-latency-local/2025161)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jul 2025]** Using `GetServerTimeNow()` for rhythm timing "results in delays for different devices and latency", and "you have to calibrate it manually for the rhythm timings". The same thread mentions Bluetooth headphones as a suspected source of occasional latency. — [Which method of getting the time should i use for a rhythm game](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/which-method-of-getting-the-time-should-i-use-for-a-rhythm-game/3823202)
- General rhythm-game practice outside Roblox: audio, video and input delays cannot be measured separately, only in combination. The standard fix is a user-adjustable offset or tap test. — [Rhythm Quest devlog 10](https://ddrkirby.com/rhythm-quest/devlog/10.html); [bemuse PR #87](https://github.com/bemusic/bemuse/pull/87)

### Inferences
- The minimum added delay before a drum hit can be heard on legacy Sound is about one frame (input is processed and `:Play` is called in the frame's script step) plus up to one mixer step, plus device output latency. Device output latency is typically larger on Android and Bluetooth (general audio knowledge, not Roblox-sourced).
- Because per-device latency is unmeasured and varies, Laskar should ship a **user audio/input offset calibration** (tap test). It should not try to engineer the latency away.
- Hit sounds must be triggered on the client, locally. Any server round trip adds the full ping.

### Gaps
- No ms measurements of Roblox's Play-to-audible latency on any platform (PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Xbox, PlayStation) were found on the DevForum or in docs.
- No official statement of the mixer buffer size or sample rate per platform.
- The cause of the June 2026 40-100 ms offset reports is unresolved.

## 3. Does the new audio API (AudioPlayer, Wire, AudioDeviceOutput, AudioFader...) improve timing or latency? Scheduled or sample-accurate playback? Status in 2026?

### Takeaway
Yes, for **sync and jitter, not raw latency**. Since early 2026, `AudioPlayer:Play(atTime)` and `Stop(atTime)`, scheduled against `SoundService:GetMixerTime()`, give documented **"sample-accurate, framerate-independent"** starts and stops, and the docs explicitly cite rhythm games. Legacy `Sound` has no equivalent. The new API left beta on Sep 10, 2024. Scheduling only works for times in the future, so it helps music start, music stop and track transitions. It does not help reactive hit sounds.

### Cited Findings
- **[DOC]** `AudioPlayer:Play(atTime: number?) -> number?`: "When `atTime` is provided, the action is scheduled against `GetMixerTime()` for sample-accurate, framerate-independent timing". The docs present this as useful for rhythm games and beat-aligned audio. With `atTime`, the call returns a unique ID for `Cancel()`. `Stop(atTime)` works the same way, for beat-synced stops and track transitions. `Cancel(actionId)` returns true if the pending action was cancelled, and false if it already happened or was never scheduled. `Play` replicates server to client. — [AudioPlayer.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/AudioPlayer.yaml); [AudioPlayer reference](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/AudioPlayer)
- **[DOC]** `SoundService:GetMixerTime()` "Returns a number in seconds that increases as the audio engine produces output. Because this clock is derived from the number of audio samples the mixer has processed, it is stable, sample-accurate, and monotonically increasing, which makes it suitable for scheduling audible changes at precise moments." It is "not suitable for synchronizing audio across sessions or networks." — [SoundService.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SoundService.yaml)
- **[DOC]** Other AudioPlayer facts. `IsReady` means "loaded, buffered, and ready to play", and it can flip back to false under extreme memory pressure when assets are unloaded. With `AutoLoad = false`, the asset loads on the first play attempt. The `Looped` event does **not** fire when you loop manually by setting TimePosition. `PlaybackSpeed` ranges from 0 to 20. `LoopRegion` and `PlaybackRegion` take NumberRange values in seconds. — [AudioPlayer.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/AudioPlayer.yaml)
- **[STAFF, Mar 31 2026]** Scheduled playback appeared in the previous week's release notes, but its flags were still off pending bug fixes. By Jun 11, 2026 users describe GetMixerTime as shipped. — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[STAFF, Oct 13 2026]** Recommended pattern: use AudioPlayer, take `SoundService:GetMixerTime()`, pick a start slightly in the future (example: now + 0.1 s), and call `Play(startAt)` on each AudioPlayer. They then start "at an exact, sample-accurate time". This is described as shipped earlier in 2026. — [2446731](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-way-to-sync-audio-tracks-together/2446731)
- **[STAFF, Sep 12 2025]** Before scheduling shipped: "perfect sync is currently not possible; however, it is something we want to enable in the near future." — [2446731](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-way-to-sync-audio-tracks-together/2446731)
- **[STAFF, Apr 20 2026]** If the planning time is very short, scheduling falls back to "play now". A batch API (`SoundService:AtOnce`) was discussed, with a warning that large batches could stall the mixer and glitch. AtOnce does **not** appear in the current SoundService docs. — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239); [SoundService.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SoundService.yaml)
- **[ANECDOTE, Apr 16 2026]** Scheduling works for syncing tracks after a short delay, but not for instantaneous sync on play. — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jan 2025]** Before scheduling shipped, the new audio API alone did not fix multi-track sync. — [2446731](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-way-to-sync-audio-tracks-together/2446731)
- **[STAFF / official account, Sep 10 2024]** "The API has officially exited Studio Beta and is now available in Studio." The post added `AudioPlayer.Volume`, so no separate AudioFader is needed for volume. Connections use Wires. The post says nothing about timing, latency or deprecating `Sound`. — [Roblox Audio API Exits Beta](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-audio-api-exits-beta-enhanced-sound-controls-now-available/3153454)
- **[SNIPPET]** The beta announcement thread dates to around Feb 2024 (a result titled "[Update] February 28, 2024"). — [New Audio API [Beta]](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-audio-api-beta-elevate-sound-and-voice-in-your-experiences/2848873). A later PSA says internal AudioPlayer playback volume "will soon double", so crossfade levels may need re-checking. — [PSA thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/psa-internal-playback-volume-for-audioplayers-will-soon-double/3907391). A beta user reported that when about 57 audios were loaded at once, not all produced output. — [beta thread post 475](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-audio-api-beta-elevate-sound-and-voice-in-your-experiences/2848873/475)
- **[DOC]** Sound instances inserted from the Creator Store "don't have the same dynamic functionality as AudioPlayer objects". — [Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets)

### Inferences
- **The strongest fix for Laskar's music sync** follows directly from the docs. Migrate the music and the "Semangat" layer to two `AudioPlayer`s (wired to an `AudioDeviceOutput`, or through `AudioFader`s for the crossfade). At round start, convert the server start time into a mixer-time target: `mixerStart = GetMixerTime() + ((nextBarServerTime - GetServerTimeNow()))`, with at least about 0.1 s of lead. Call `Play(mixerStart)` on **both** players. After that, never seek. The two loops then start on the same sample, and crossfading is only a volume change.
- After the scheduled start, the local music position can be computed as `(GetMixerTime() - mixerStart) % 16`. This is a sample-accurate local clock of what the mixer has rendered. It excludes the device output delay, so a calibration offset is still needed. It avoids reading TimePosition entirely.
- GetMixerTime and GetServerTimeNow are different clocks. GetMixerTime follows the audio device's sample clock, and GetServerTimeNow is a network-synced wall clock. Over long sessions they may drift by small amounts, since audio device clocks typically differ from system clocks by parts-per-million (general audio knowledge, not Roblox-sourced). Over a 16 s loop or a few-minute round this is likely negligible. For long sessions, re-anchor occasionally at a loop boundary using a scheduled `Stop` and `Play`, not by seeking.
- For drum hits, scheduling cannot help, because a hit cannot be scheduled into the past. The new API does not document lower output latency than `Sound`. The choice between `Sound` and `AudioPlayer` for hits is mostly about convenience and routing.

### Gaps
- No documentation of whether `atTime` scheduling is honored on all platforms equally, or what minimum lead time is required before it falls back to "play now".
- No statement on whether AudioPlayer has lower Play-to-audible latency than `Sound`.
- No official deprecation timeline for legacy `Sound`. None was found.
- No exact release-notes version or date for when `Play(atTime)` and `GetMixerTime` were fully enabled. Evidence brackets it between late March and June 2026.

## 4. Seamless looping (Looped gaps, LoopRegion and PlaybackRegion), preloading (PreloadAsync, IsLoaded), avoiding first-play delay

### Takeaway
Engine-side looping (`Sound.Looped` or `AudioPlayer.Looping`, optionally with `LoopRegion`) is the right tool. Never loop by writing TimePosition. LoopRegion and PlaybackRegion work on `Sound` only when `PlaybackRegionsEnabled = true` (enabled by staff in June 2023), and they are native on AudioPlayer. Preload with `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync` and check `IsLoaded`/`IsReady`. Even preloaded audio can be evicted from RAM under memory pressure, and some first-play and clone delays persist (mostly reported in Studio).

### Cited Findings
- **[DOC]** `Sound.Looped` sets whether the sound repeats, and `DidLoop(soundId, numOfTimesLooped)` counts the loops. `LoopRegion` is a loop start/end range "within the PlaybackRegion, in seconds". `PlaybackRegion` is a start/stop range within TimeLength, inactive if Min == Max. Both require `PlaybackRegionsEnabled = true`. — [Sound.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Sound.yaml)
- **[DOC]** AudioPlayer has `Looping`, `LoopRegion` (clamped to PlaybackRegion, falling back to PlaybackRegion if Min == Max), `PlaybackRegion`, and a `Looped` event that does not fire for manual TimePosition loops. — [AudioPlayer.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/AudioPlayer.yaml)
- **[STAFF, Jun 28 2023]** The LoopRegion/PlaybackRegion API on `Sound` was enabled on June 27, 2023, after being documented but non-functional since at least November 2022. Before that, a common workaround was to schedule the next sound at `TimeLength - ~0.02 s` from a script, which the poster called "very hacky". — [2440706](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/soundplaybackregionsenabled-soundplaybackregion-soundloopregion-do-nothing/2440706)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jun 2020, possibly outdated]** A looped Sound had "a few ms delay" before restarting, which desynced loops from animations. The poster compared Roblox unfavorably with Unity and Unreal. — [More Precise Audio Timing Ability](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-precise-audio-timing-ability-and-other-sound-engine-feature-requests/650609)
- **[DOC]** `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync(instances, callback)` "Yields until all of the assets associated with the given Instances have loaded", including Sounds. It does not error on failures, and the callback reports an `AssetFetchStatus` per asset. The docs advise preloading only essential assets. Items enter the request queue "when an asset is used for the first time or PreloadAsync() is called". — [ContentProvider.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/ContentProvider.yaml)
- **[DOC]** `Sound.IsLoaded` is true "when the Sound has loaded from Roblox servers and is ready to play". `Loaded` fires once, so check `IsLoaded` before waiting on it. — [Sound.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Sound.yaml). `AudioPlayer.IsReady` means "loaded, buffered, and ready to play" and can revert to false under memory pressure. — [AudioPlayer.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/AudioPlayer.yaml)
- **[ANECDOTE + developer analysis, Mar 2025]** The engine appears to keep less audio in RAM than before. Evicted sounds stay cached on disk, so they are not re-downloaded, but re-triggering them can still lag. Using **one file per sound**, not multi-note "sprite" files that are seeked into, gave smoother playback. Preloading did not fix the delay for combined files. — [3548917](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/heavy-soundinput-lag-in-piano-games/3548917)
- **[ANECDOTE, May 2024]** One poster reported that preloading all audio IDs did not remove a ~0.1 s delay, then later said it was Studio-only. — [2961664](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/playing-a-sound-by-any-means-and-playonremove-have-a-01-second-delay/2961664)
- **[MEASURED, 2022, fixed]** Upload processing once appended a random amount of trailing silence (2-22 ms), which broke loop lengths. It was fixed on May 12, 2022. — [1735440](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recent-changes-to-uploaded-audio-compressionprocessing-causing-timing-drift/1735440)

### Inferences
- For the 16 s loop, check in code that `TimeLength` is 16.000 s (± a few ms). If it isn't, set `LoopRegion = NumberRange.new(0, 16)` (with `PlaybackRegionsEnabled = true` on Sound) so any encoder padding at the end is excluded. Lossy transcoding commonly pads, though Roblox's codec is undocumented.
- Preload both music layers and all drum samples with `PreloadAsync` behind the round's countdown. Start music only when `IsLoaded` (Sound) or `IsReady` (AudioPlayer) is true. Keep drum Sound instances alive for the whole session, not created on demand, to reduce eviction and first-play hitches.
- Since a staff member confirmed that Ended and loop signals land on the first frame after the event, beat logic should never be driven off `DidLoop` or `Ended` timing.

### Gaps
- No current (2025-2026) measurement of whether `Sound.Looped` or `AudioPlayer.Looping` still introduces any gap at the loop point. The only gap report is from 2020.
- Roblox's internal codec, transcoded sample rate, and any encoder delay or padding are not documented.
- No official RAM budget for decoded audio, or eviction policy, is published.

## 5. Techniques for syncing gameplay to music: TimePosition as master vs os.clock() or GetServerTimeNow(), smoothing, PlaybackSpeed, lag spikes and frame drops

### Takeaway
Pre-2026 community practice was either to read TimePosition directly, or to anchor an `os.clock()` clock at play start and correct it occasionally. Roblox staff recommend **gentle PlaybackSpeed nudging** (for example ±5% when over 100 ms off), not seeking, for legacy `Sound`. Since 2026 they recommend **scheduled AudioPlayer starts on GetMixerTime**. Frame drops degrade any frame-sampled clock, which matters at 30 FPS and below.

### Cited Findings
- **[STAFF, Jul 7 2025]** Drift-correction recipe: compare positions in a loop. If more than **0.1 s** ahead, set PlaybackSpeed to **0.95**. If more than 0.1 s behind, set it to **1.05**. Otherwise use 1.0. Tune the values so the pitch change isn't noticeable. The original poster reported "really good results". — [3800504](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/syncing-sounds-with-timeposition-isnt-reliable-anymore-glitches-and-stuttery-sounds/3800504)
- **[STAFF, Mar 13 2026]** Again suggested nudging PlaybackSpeed based on drift, noting it shifts pitch. **[ANECDOTE, Mar 31 2026]** One developer found that speed adjustment caused audible distortion. **[ANECDOTE, Apr 2026]** A stopgap used Actors (parallel Luau) with PlaybackSpeed correction, tagged via CollectionService. — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)
- **[SNIPPET]** An alternative nudge uses thresholds around 0.001 with a 0.01 speed adjustment, which avoids rebuffering. — summarized in search results for [3800504](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/syncing-sounds-with-timeposition-isnt-reliable-anymore-glitches-and-stuttery-sounds/3800504). The specific numbers were not found in the fetched page and may come from a different reply or thread.
- **[SNIPPET]** Another approach is to record `os.clock()` when playback starts and compute elapsed time as the song position, since it is high precision. — [How to get time position of sound?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-get-time-position-of-sound/2650417)
- **[SNIPPET]** `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` is a Unix timestamp with millisecond precision that client and server agree on. — [tick() appears to be outputting the incorrect time](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tick-appears-to-be-outputting-the-incorrect-time-on-the-roblox-client/3273097). **[ANECDOTE, Jul 2025]** A rhythm-game developer found GetServerTimeNow "seems to change based on latency and the performance of the device" and needed manual calibration. The suggested approach is to use the Sound's position for the song timeline and offset late joiners by an averaged ping (`player:GetNetworkPing()`). — [3823202](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/which-method-of-getting-the-time-should-i-use-for-a-rhythm-game/3823202)
- **[SNIPPET]** "When performance drops, RenderStepped will call less frequently, resulting in a slightly offbeat feel that makes it nearly impossible to match the rhythm when the framerate drops to or below 30FPS." — [Are there more efficient ways to keep rhythm? (RunService)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/are-there-more-efficient-ways-to-keep-rhythm-runservice/2173574)
- **[SNIPPET]** `task.wait` overshoots the requested time, with one report of a minimum of about 29 ms, so wait-based beat scheduling is imprecise. — [Task.wait() is very inconsistent](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taskwait-is-very-inconsistent/3030119). An older "Aligned Waiting" module admits it still depends on `wait()`. — [Aligned Waiting Module](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/aligned-waiting-module-a-more-accurate-way-to-yield-in-scripts/560027)
- **[SNIPPET]** A beat-indicator implementation jumped ahead "4 bars" when tempo or beat-steps changed, showing a common bug in recomputing the beat phase. — [This "Beat Indicator" is Inaccurate](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/this-beat-indicator-is-inaccurate-whenever-beat-steps-change/2179774)
- **[SNIPPET]** A common judge method is to tag each note with a song time and compare it with TimePosition at input. — [How would you make accuracy system](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-would-you-make-accuracy-system/1279543)
- **[DOC]** TimePosition advances at PlaybackSpeed, so any song clock must multiply elapsed time by PlaybackSpeed. — [Sound.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Sound.yaml)

### Inferences
- **Recommended clock design for Laskar** (synthesis, not a single source):
  1. **Shared truth:** keep the server start attribute plus `GetServerTimeNow()` to decide which beat and bar the squad is on. This is needed for multiplayer agreement.
  2. **Music follows the clock once, not continuously.** Schedule the music start with `AudioPlayer:Play(atTime)` at the mixer time that matches the next bar boundary (see Q3). If legacy `Sound` is kept, replace the "snap if over 30 ms" rule with PlaybackSpeed nudging (for example ±1-2% when over about 20-40 ms off, ±5% when over 100 ms). Hard-seek only beyond a large threshold (for example 150-250 ms, after a lag spike or a backgrounded app), preferably at a loop boundary.
  3. **Judge hits against a local, smoothed clock.** Use `os.clock()` anchored to the music start, or `GetMixerTime()` on the AudioPlayer path, plus a user calibration offset. Do not use raw TimePosition sampled on a frame, since that adds up to one frame of quantization at low FPS.
  4. **Input timestamps:** judge with the time at which input was processed, not the next RenderStepped. Input timing itself is out of scope here.
- Measure drift with a filter, not a single sample. For example, average `TimePosition - expected` over several frames, or take the minimum of the last N samples, to remove frame-sampling noise before deciding to correct. One reading per second with a 30 ms threshold is noise-prone.
- On a lag spike or a long frame, do not try to catch audio up immediately. The mixer keeps playing during script stalls (the engine runs concurrently with scripts per staff), so the audio is usually still right. Only the visuals are late.

### Gaps
- No open-source, maintained Roblox rhythm-game conductor module with documented accuracy measurements was found in this pass.
- No measurement of how much GetServerTimeNow jitters or steps on the client over time. There is only the anecdote that it "changes based on latency and performance".
- No data on how PlaybackSpeed changes are applied (immediately, or at the next mixer step) or whether they cause clicks.

## 6. Polyphony and voice limits, cost of many short sounds, and reusing one Sound vs cloning per hit

### Takeaway
No official voice limit is documented. Community reports range from a soft limit around 50-65 audible voices (older, around 2020) to "1-400 normal" in a 2025 single-PC benchmark. A rhythm game's 4 drums plus 2 music layers are far below any reported limit. **Cloning a Sound per hit is the documented pain point**: a 2025-2026 bug, still open in Oct 2026, gives clones a noticeable start delay (mostly in Studio), with streaming-buffer refill as the staff's hypothesis. Pooling or reusing pre-loaded instances is the community workaround.

### Cited Findings
- **[DOC]** Sound and SoundService references state no simultaneous-voice cap. — [Sound.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Sound.yaml); [SoundService.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SoundService.yaml)
- **[MEASURED, Jun 14 2025, single PC: i5-12500H, 16 GB]** Benchmark of synchronized sound instances: 1-400 gave "normal operation". 401-450 gave slight AV desync. 451-500 desynced after 5-7 s. 501-2000 cut out after 2-4 s. Above that it cut out faster, and the PC froze at 300k+. "Results may vary depending on the device." Not staff. — [Total sound instance limit](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/total-sound-instance-limit/3736250)
- **[SNIPPET, older, around 2020]** With more than about 50 playing Sounds the volume was lowered, and past about 65 it became nearly 0. One reply speculated that FMOD limits playback to 50 (or 100) voices. — [How do I prevent this weird sound behavior when there are a lot of playing sounds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-prevent-this-weird-sound-behavior-when-there-are-a-lot-of-playing-sounds/927669). **[SNIPPET, around 2022, author unverified]** "changes were made to increase the number of voices and smarter managing of sound loading and unloading". — [Sound Engine Broken - Choppy / Nonexistent Sounds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sound-engine-broken-choppy-nonexistent-sounds/1663035/35)
- **[ANECDOTE, Jun 2025]** Shooter games can reach hundreds of sounds in full servers. One developer uses distance culling and a "heat & queue" priority system and says "sounds take up more performance room than you'd think". — [3736250](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/total-sound-instance-limit/3736250)
- **[ANECDOTE + STAFF, Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, unresolved]** Cloned Sounds take a while to start, with reports of "about a second" (Aug 2026) and "noticeable where timing matters". The delay is mainly in Studio, and some users say not in the live game. It especially affects sub-second sounds triggered rapidly, such as footsteps. Staff DinoCoderMom could not reproduce it at first ("press C rapid fire and it plays every sound without delay"), then logged it to the backlog (Apr 2026). Staff ReallyLongArms (Oct 5, 2026) hypothesized that "we stream larger audio files" and "each new clone would need to refill that buffered window", expecting it to matter less for short sounds. Community workaround: "pooling seems to be the only solution". — [Random Delay on Cloned Sound Instances](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/random-delay-on-cloned-sound-instances/4079207)
- **[ANECDOTE, May 2024]** A ~0.1 s delay was attributed to server-side clone-then-destroy. Playing locally on each client via `SoundService:PlayLocalSound` was the suggested fix. Another reply suggested "loading each sound once and reusing it". — [2961664](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/playing-a-sound-by-any-means-and-playonremove-have-a-01-second-delay/2961664)
- **[DOC]** `SoundService:PlayLocalSound(sound)` "Plays a copy of a Sound locally", heard only by the calling client. The copy carries Volume, TimePosition, PlaybackSpeed and effects, but not Looped. — [SoundService.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SoundService.yaml)
- **[STAFF, Mar 2026]** Short files are fully decompressed in RAM, so seeking them (for example `TimePosition = 0`) is "nearly instant". — [4518239](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allow-us-to-continuously-sync-timeposition-of-2-sound-objects/4518239)

### Inferences
- Laskar's current hit approach (one persistent Sound per drum, `TimePosition = 0; :Play()`, client-side) is consistent with the evidence. Short drum WAVs should be in the "fully decompressed" class, so the rewind is cheap, and no cloning occurs. Its one drawback: restarting the same instance **cuts off the previous hit's tail** when the same drum is hit quickly. At 120 BPM with on-beat hits, the gap between same-drum hits is at least 0.5 s, unless half-beat hits are allowed. If tails ring longer, use a round-robin pool of 2-3 pre-created, preloaded Sounds per drum. Do not clone.
- `PlayLocalSound` creates a copy internally ("Plays a copy"). Whether that copy pays the clone-delay cost is unknown, so for minimum latency prefer calling `:Play()` on a persistent instance.
- Trim leading silence from the drum WAVs before upload, since any silence before the transient adds directly to perceived latency. Make the attack start at sample 0.
- Voice count (4-12 hit voices plus 2 music layers) is negligible compared with any reported threshold.

### Gaps
- No official voice limit or voice-stealing policy (by priority, distance or volume) is documented.
- No measured per-`:Play()` CPU cost, and no comparison of `:Play()` on a persistent instance vs `PlayLocalSound` vs AudioPlayer for hit latency.
- Whether the clone-delay bug affects live clients on mobile or console is not confirmed either way.

## 7. Audio upload constraints (formats, length, monthly limits, moderation, sample rate) as of 2026

### Takeaway
Uploads accept mp3, ogg, wav and flac. Each file must be under 20 MB and 7 minutes, at most 48 kHz, and mono, stereo, 3.0 or 5.1. ID-verified creators get 2,000 free uploads per 30 days, unverified creators 100. Studio transcodes on import, and audio must pass moderation before reuse. No moderation timeframe or output codec is documented.

### Cited Findings
- **[DOC]** Formats: ".mp3, .ogg, .wav or .flac", single track or stream. Size and length: "less than 20 MB in size and 7 minutes in duration". Sample rate: "less than or equal to 48 kHz". Channels: "mono or stereo 2.0, 3.0, or 5.1 surround". — [Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets)
- **[DOC]** Limits: ID-verified creators get "2,000 free audio assets per 30 days", unverified creators "100 free audio assets per 30 days". Any older daily limit was replaced by this monthly limit. Uploads are free (no per-asset price listed). — [Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets)
- **[DOC]** Studio **transcodes** imported audio before saving it, and import errors are usually transcoding rejections. Old audio tools can produce invalid headers or frames. Audio must "pass moderation" before reuse in other projects, with no timeframe given. Assets are private by default, and permissions can be granted to specific experiences or friends. Uploads are classified as sound effects or songs. — [Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets)
- **[MEASURED, 2022, fixed]** Server-side processing once changed clip lengths by adding random trailing silence (+2.2 to +12.3 ms per clip, and about 22 ms between "equal" loops). Fixed May 12, 2022. — [1735440](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recent-changes-to-uploaded-audio-compressionprocessing-causing-timing-drift/1735440)

### Inferences
- Laskar's WAV uploads are within limits. A 16 s 44.1 or 48 kHz stereo 16-bit WAV is about 2.8-3.1 MB. Because transcoding is lossy or opaque, verify after upload that `TimeLength` and the attack onset still line up with the beat grid. The project already has beat-alignment tests for the soundfont-rendered music, so extend them to the uploaded asset.
- Use 48 kHz or 44.1 kHz sources. Above 48 kHz is rejected.
- Note the music layers' asset IDs and permission grants, since private-by-default assets must be granted to the experience. This matters if the place is moved to a group.

### Gaps
- Roblox's transcoded codec, bitrate, output sample rate and encoder delay or padding are not documented.
- No documented moderation turnaround time.
- No current statement on whether file size or duration affects which loading strategy (in-RAM vs streamed) an asset gets. Per the March 2026 staff explanation this matters for seek behavior, but no thresholds are published.
